# Fonts

Halite's editor bundles two typefaces as JUCE binary data (`HaliteAssets`, `plugins/halite/CMakeLists.txt`).
Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; `OFL.txt` holds both licences with their copyright
lines. The files are byte-for-byte the official release files, unmodified; they are not sold on
their own and keep their reserved font names.
