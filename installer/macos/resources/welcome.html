Choosing what to install

The next pages show one row per plugin. Install or update is ticked for plugins that are missing or older on this Mac and unticked for those already up to date; open a row to pick formats. Remove lists the plugins found on this Mac; ticking one deletes all its formats. Your presets and settings stay untouched either way.

If macOS would not open this installer

The installer is not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS asks once before opening it.

  1. Double-click the installer. When macOS says it was not opened, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password. The installer opens.

Or, in Terminal: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/MyzelAudio-*.pkg, then open it. The plugins it installs need no further step.
